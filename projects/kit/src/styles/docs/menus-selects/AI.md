# AI notes: Menus and selects

- Use Material's `mat-select`, `mat-menu`, `mat-autocomplete` as they are: the kit styles them.
- Form fields are `appearance="outline"`.
- Options with descriptions: `panelClass="lenzi-described-options"` and the two classes; nothing else.
- Menu items: an icon, then a whole word. A destructive item goes last and asks first.
